What Is a Crypto Launchpad?

A crypto launchpad is a platform where new blockchain projects raise funds by selling tokens in a structured, transparent presale environment. It’s where early-stage builders meet early-stage believers – giving both sides an edge before the project goes public.

  • For creators, a token launchpad isn’t just about funding. It’s about credibility. Launchpads filter out low-effort projects, so getting listed signals that you’re serious. You also get instant exposure, access to engaged investors, and built-in marketing support.
  • For investors, launchpad crypto projects offer better prices and better terms than the open market. You’re getting in early – before the hype, before the markups. Top crypto launchpad platforms also vet projects, helping reduce risk and weed out scams.

And because launchpads typically cap contributions per wallet, token distribution stays fair. No whales draining the pool. No bots front-running the sale. Just real access for real backers.

The best launchpads for crypto also build a strong community around each project – giving tokens momentum before they ever hit an exchange. Whether you're launching or investing, a crypto launchpad gives you a smarter way to start.

Why Are Launchpads Important for Presales & IDOs?

Presales lay the foundation for a crypto project’s success. Take for example, $NEX on Uniswap, during this stage the initial funding is raised, as well as engaged community. Now this project has raised more than $7.6 M, successfully passed Certik and Solid Proof audits, all due to a carefully curated start.

This is where crypto launchpads come in. They give presales and Initial DEX Offerings (IDOs) the structure they need to work. Think of a launchpad for crypto as both a filter and a megaphone: it keeps the bad actors out and helps legit projects get seen.

A strong crypto launchpad platform handles vetting, wallet caps, KYC, and smart contract auditing – turning what could be a chaotic free-for-all into a secure, fair token sale. Without that layer of trust, even great projects struggle to attract serious early investors. The difference shows in the outcomes.

  • Binance Launchpad gave early exposure to projects like Matic Network (Polygon) and Fetch.ai – both now major players with billions in market cap.
  • Polkastarter helped launch dozens of gaming and DeFi IDOs that now sit among the top launchpad crypto projects by ROI.

Launchpads solve the core pain points of early crypto fundraising:

  • They reduce risk through vetting and transparent smart contracts.
  • They enable fair token distribution – no whales, no bots, no insider drain.
  • They build early traction by connecting projects to active, aligned communities.

For both sides of the equation – builders and backers – presale launchpad crypto platforms are no longer optional. They’re where serious projects launch and where informed investors look first.

The 11 Best IDO Platforms for Crypto Projects

These are the crypto launchpad projects that are actually moving the space forward. Each one brings something unique to the table – from stronger vetting processes to deeper liquidity and multi-chain reach. If you’re serious about launching (or investing in) legit crypto projects, here are the top crypto launchpads you need to know.

1. DAO Maker – IDO Launchpad

DAO Maker is designed for retail. It opens up early-stage investing to individuals, not just whales or insiders. DAO Maker also integrates risk mitigation through community governance and token-based reputation systems.

This IDO platform focuses on lowering risk and increasing access. It’s already integrated with major names like 1inch, Wanchain, and Ronin. Notable launches: Orion Protocol, My Neighbor Alice, XCAD Network. Average ROI (as of 2024): ~15x at ATH, ~4x average.

  • Total DAO supply: 278,306,660.33 DAO
  • Targets small-to-mid cap launchpad crypto projects
  • Emphasizes risk reduction through DAO participation
  • The platform offers two main types of token sales: Public SHO (Strong Holder Offering) and Private SHO
  • Offers Venture Bond, where investors stake their digital assets and get a guaranteed return on their investments

To participate, users must create an account on the DAO Maker website, complete KYC verification, and connect a supported crypto wallet. Access to token sales typically requires staking DAO tokens to earn DAO Power, which affects allocation eligibility.

2. Polkastarter – IDO Launchpad

Polkastarter is built for seamless cross-chain token pools and has become a favorite for launching GameFi, NFT, and DeFi projects. Projects go through an internal vetting process, and token sales are conducted through permissionless, decentralized pools.

It supports Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, and Avalanche. Notable launches include: Astra Guild Ventures, Highstreet, WonderHero.

  • Community-driven allocation through POLS token staking
  • 108 projects have been successfully funded on the platform, with $48,582,502.04 raised
  • The project is backed by: Astronaut | Capital, KYROS VENTURES, Moonrock Capital

To take part in IDO listings on Polkastarter, users need to either provide liquidity or hold a set amount of POLS tokens in their wallet. At present, the requirement is holding 250 POLS for at least seven days.

3. Binance Launchpad – IEO Launchpad

This launchpad project crypto needs no introduction. All projects go through a rigorous due diligence process before conducting an Initial Exchange Offering (IEO). Investors need to hold BNB to participate. Projects receive immediate liquidity and visibility due to listing on Binance post-launch. You might know this one from launches of Polygon (MATIC), Axie Infinity (AXS), Band Protocol, Fetch.ai.

  • Uses the IEO model, backed by Binance’s massive user base
  • Binance Launchpad projects list includes 76 launched crypto startups with over $523M funds raised.
  • Strict vetting = low scam risk
  • Strong liquidity post-listing

To participate in Binance Launchpad, users must stake a minimum of 0.1 BNB. The staked BNB is locked during the subscription period, and once it ends, users receive token allocations based on their committed amount.

4. Seedify – IDO Launchpad

Seedify is all about early-stage innovation. Anyone can submit a project, and the community votes to approve IDOs. Its notable launches: Bloktopia, SinVerse, ChainGuardians. The launchpad uses a DAO-like model where SFUND token holders vote on project listings. Seedify uses a 9-tier system to determine IDO allocation, access to project incentives, and private sale opportunities. Users need a minimum of 500 $SFUND to enter Tier 1, while reaching the top tier requires holding 100,000 $SFUND. It also offers incubation, marketing support, and cross-chain integrations. It's fast becoming a go-to for blockchain apps and tools.

  • Community governance drives funding decisions
  • Early access to new launchpad crypto projects
  • Integrated with PancakeSwap, Solana, and Avalanche

The native token of Seedify, $SFUND, serves multiple purposes. By staking $SFUND, holders can access IDO participation on the platform. It also allows users to buy and own exclusive NFTs and digital assets from supported projects.

5. Gate.io – IEO Launchpad

A feature of the Gate.io exchange, this platform curates token sales with tight KYC/AML rules. It’s built for investors who want security and legitimacy. Projects like Evmos, Conflux, KardiaChain went through tight KYC/AML screening. Users can purchase tokens via crypto or fiat, making it ideal for beginners. Immediate exchange listing ensures liquidity from day one.

  • Easy fiat or crypto purchase options
  • Strong compliance = higher investor trust
  • IEO model allows direct exchange listing

To join Gate.io Startup, users must complete KYC verification. Guaranteed share allocations are available to those with VIP level 1 or above, which depends on GT holdings and 30-day trading volume. Participants also need at least $10 in their account—paid in GT for new listings.

6. BSCPad – IDO Launchpad

BSCPad brings structured access to Binance Smart Chain projects. Projects launched: GameZone, VelasPad, MetaVPad. It removes the usual headaches of staking or random selection by guaranteeing tier-based allocations.

  • Dual-round system to ensure wide distribution
  • Full-stack support (legal, dev, tokenomics)
  • Built for early-stage DeFi and utility tokens

The $BSCPAD token powers the entire BSCPad launchpad ecosystem and currently holds a market cap exceeding $3 million. Beyond the token, the platform runs an incubation arm known as BlueZilla, which supports early-stage projects—from initial concept through launch—while helping them build and grow an engaged community.

7. BullPerks – IDO Launchpad

BullPerks runs on a 6-tier system and supports projects on Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, and BSC. It's especially strong in crypto gaming IDOs and NFT launches. It’s one of the few launchpads offering continued marketing and advisory post-launch.

  • More than 65 projects successfully launched
  • Long-term support across launch and post-launch
  • 130.83x ATH ROI
  • Funding Secured $15 million
  • High transparency and investor engagement
  • 300M total tokens BLP

8. GamesPad – Gaming IDO Launchpad

GamesPad focuses on one thing: gaming. From NFT ecosystems to metaverse integrations, this IDO platform targets projects in the GameFi vertical. Notable projects: AquaFarm, Reign of Terror. The platform supports ETH, BSC, and Polychain networks.

  • Community-first approach to fundraising
  • Strict project curation = higher upside potential
  • Backed by: BullPerks

GamesPad features a 5-tier investing system where each tier is symbolized by a unique NFT that sets your allocation size. To obtain a tier, you must purchase the corresponding GamesPad NFT along with the required number of $GMPD tokens. GMPD token holders can participate in every deal, with their level of access and allocation determined by how many tokens they’ve staked or locked. This approach ensures fair participation across all tiers.

9. KuCoin Spotlight – IEO Launchpad

KuCoin’s launchpad Spotlight gives startups direct access to a massive trading audience. With tight due diligence and full KYC/AML, it’s one of the more regulated token launchpad routes. It has rigorous vetting, full compliance enforcement, and immediate liquidity upon token listing. Projects like LUKSO, Tokoin, ClearDAO were launched here.

  • Seamless IEO process with immediate exchange listing
  • Strong investor protections
  • Curated projects only – no open submissions

To participate in the token sale, users are required to complete KYC verification and hold KuCoin Token (KCS). Allocation is determined based on the Net Asset Value (NAV) of each user's token holdings.

10. GameFi.org – Gaming IDO Launchpad

GameFi is the go-to crypto launchpad for gaming and fantasy sports. Launches: Pegaxy, DeathRoad, Step Hero. It supports fast transactions, detailed on-chain activity tracking, and player-friendly design.

  • Built on Solana for speed and scale
  • Offers up to 51% dividend return to stakeholders
  • Total Funds Raised $15M
  • Gamified token economies + NFT integration

To join a token sale on GameFi.org, start by connecting your wallet. Next, stake $GAFI at to earn at least the Rookie rank, which is the minimum requirement to participate. The launchpad uses a rank-based system, and users must hold one of the following ranks: Rookie, Elite, Pro, or Legend. After staking, complete the KYC process and allow 24 to 48 hours for verification. Once approved, submit your whitelist application to secure your spot in the sale.

11. TrustPad – IDO Launchpad

Built on Binance Smart Chain but multi-chain ready, TrustPad provides safe, fair fundraising across Ethereum, Solana, and more. Besides its native token, TrustPad supports farming in token pairs paired with $TPAD on the platform. However, starting February 28, 2023, TrustPad announced that LP pools are no longer eligible to participate in IDOs.

  • Low-friction onboarding for projects
  • It successfully passed security audits by code auditors such as Certik and Quill Audits.
  • Users who stake a certain amount of TrustPad tokens (e.g., 15,000 $TPAD) are guaranteed an allocation in IDOs.
  • Offers lottery system for users staking a smaller amount of $TPAD (e.g., 3,000) are entered into a lottery for a chance to win an allocation
  • Multi-chain: BSC, ETH, Solana, Polkadot, Polygon

You can stake TPAD tokens in an IDO pool or an IDO LP pool to earn rewards. You can stake TPAD directly in an IDO pool for periods ranging from 14 to 1,095 days on the staking page. Alternatively, create a TPAD-BNB liquidity pool (LP) and stake it in the IDO LP staking pool to earn passive income in TPAD while benefiting from the returns of the projects you support.

How to Choose the Right Launchpad for Your Project

Choosing the best crypto launchpad can make or break your project. With hundreds of platforms available, it’s tempting to chase trends or go with the loudest marketing. Don’t. The right launchpad in crypto should align with your project’s goals, audience, and tech stack. Here’s what to look for when reviewing any crypto launchpad list:

  • Reputation & Track Record. Stick with top launchpad crypto platforms that have successfully launched similar projects. Review community feedback, past project performance, and how actively the platform supports post-launch growth. Whether you're launching a utility token, DeFi protocol, or NFT-based game, trust is everything.
  • Security & Vetting. Not all crypto presale launchpad options offer real protection. Strong IDO launchpad or IEO launchpad platforms do due diligence on every project, helping investors feel safe and boosting your credibility. Look for built-in KYC/AML processes and anti-scam measures.
  • Tokenomics Support. Your launchpad crypto project needs the right distribution model – tiers, vesting, and allocations that protect your token’s value. The best crypto launchpad platforms provide advisory support and proven frameworks to help you scale sustainably.
  • Chain Compatibility. Make sure the launchpad for crypto supports your blockchain – Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana, Polygon, etc. Cross-chain or multi-chain launchpads give you flexibility and access to wider audiences.
  • Cost vs. Value. The best launchpads aren’t always the cheapest. Evaluate fees, token share, and services offered. Do you get marketing, advisory, or post-launch liquidity support? The best launchpads for DeFi token presales go beyond just fundraising.
  • Community Access. From free crypto launchpads to tier-based IDOs, the model matters. Choose a platform that aligns with your ideal investor base. Do they offer fair access or reward early backers? Are they known for engaging, long-term communities?

If you’re comparing new launchpad crypto options or exploring the best crypto IDO platforms for 2025, focus on fit – not flash. Review the crypto launchpad list with a clear head. Projects that do well on the right token launchpad aren't just lucky – they’re strategic.
