What Are ICOs and Why Do They Matter in 2025

An initial coin offering or ICO involves publically selling project tokens to raise funds for its further development. In turn, in addition to the potential for growth in the external market value of tokens, token holders receive certain privileges (whether it be access to DAO, platform functionality, etc.). Considering the well-developed regulatory environment that accompanies current ICOs, today, with proper analysis, this is an excellent way to raise money quickly on the recent ICO coins and become part of a promising blockchain project.

How We Curated This ICO List

Our team has conducted a thorough analysis of many Web3 crypto projects and realized that the aspects listed below provide the most objective assessment of their ICO’s reliability:

  • The project’s legitimacy. Each of the projects from our initial coin offerings list has been checked for registration, legal structure, AML/KYC compliance, and the presence of a third-party auditor (for example, Certik or Hacken);
  • The project team and its reputation. In our list of crypto ICOs, we gave priority to projects where the founders and technical experts have verifiable experience in large tech companies or successful Web3 startups. To do this, we checked their LinkedIn profiles, participation in other projects, as well as the presence of publications, speeches, and open-source commits;
  • Tokenomics. We carefully analyzed the new ICO coins distribution mechanics, including shares for the team, reserves, liquidity, as well as rewards for the community and investors. Particular attention during the analysis was paid to vesting mechanisms and deflationary tools;
  • Technical implementation and activity on GitHub. We expected new ICO projects to have not just a whitepaper but also an actual result, such as a ready MVP or testnet. In all cases, we checked the repositories for activity, pull requests, bug fixes, and discussions in issue tracks;
  • Community. We analyzed new ICO’s community activities in Telegram, Discord, and Reddit to find out how the crypto project team interacts with the team, how lively the discussions are, and how transparent and regular the AMA sessions are.
  • USP and its prospects. For our ICO initial coin offering list, we selected projects with innovative and scalable technology (in particular, AI + DeFi, tokenization, and private networks), existing markets, and real demand.

How to Evaluate New ICO Cryptocurrency

Even experienced ICO investors sometimes make rash decisions. To avoid this, you have to evaluate each project from your ICO crypto list by the following aspects:

  • Whitepaper (it should contain an explanation of the project’s tech aspects: its architecture, token use cases, and economic model);
  • Independent smart contract audit (a report with the edits made will be a green flag for investing);
  • Token utility (successful ICOs are s tied to a product with real use cases, and tokens have a practical application, whether it is a reward, staking, payment for gas, etc.);
  • Token distribution and liquidity mechanisms (check whether the team has plans to list the new ICO cryptocurrency on DEX or CEX and whether liquidity pools are organized).

Where to Find The Latest ICO Listings

Where to find crypto ICO, – you may ask. Generally speaking, to form your own ICO cryptocurrency list, you can use the following ICO crypto websites:

  • CoinMarketCap (in particular, the ICO/IDO section): here, you will find a list of projects with basic metrics, tokenomics, ICO release dates, and links to their official websites;
  • ICOBench, ICO Drops, ICO Hot List, and CryptoRank: these are independent crypto ICO trackers with ratings, timelines, and analytics;
  • CoinGecko and TokenInsight: these crypto ICO websites allow you to compare the tokenomics of the projects you are interested in and the parameters of their smart contracts;
  • Reddit (r/CryptoICO) and Telegram channels: there, you can track discussions of the project from crypto enthusiasts.
