Cookie Policy

At Nexchain, we keep cookie usage transparent. Learn how cookies support the website experience and how you can manage them.

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions or preferences over time. Cookies help improve your user experience by enabling the website to recognize you on subsequent visits and to provide personalized content or features.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following types of cookies on our website:

  1. Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the operation of the website and enable you to move around and use its features. Without these cookies, certain services like access to secure areas of the website cannot be provided.
  2. Performance Cookies: These cookies collect information about how visitors use the website, such as which pages are most frequently visited, and if any error messages are encountered. These cookies help us improve the performance of the website.
  3. Functionality Cookies: These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make, such as your language preferences, and provide enhanced, more personalized features. For example, they may remember your login details or language preferences.
  4. Targeting/Advertising Cookies: These cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are more relevant to you and your interests. They can also limit the number of times you see an advertisement and help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  1. Enhance your experience on the website, such as remembering your preferences and settings.
  2. Analyze website usage and performance to improve the functionality and content of our site.
  3. Provide personalized content, such as recommendations or ads, based on your browsing behavior.
  4. Ensure the security of the website and your data.

Third-Party Cookies

We may allow third-party services, such as analytics providers or advertising networks, to set cookies on your device when you visit our website. These third parties may use cookies to collect information about your online activities across different websites and over time. These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of those third parties. For example, we may use Google Analytics to track website usage and performance, and other third-party advertising networks to show relevant ads.

How to Manage Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies by adjusting the settings on your web browser. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. However, please note that if you choose to disable cookies, certain features of the website may not function properly, and your experience may be less personalized.

To learn more about how to manage cookies in your browser, you can visit the following links:

  1. Google Chrome
  2. Mozilla Firefox
  3. Safari
  4. Microsoft Edge
