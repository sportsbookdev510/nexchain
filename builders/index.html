What can you build?

We are looking for useful products that solve a clear user or developer problem and contribute to the Nexchain ecosystem.

DeFi Applications

Trading, lending, liquidity, payments, and asset-management products with added risk context.

Wallet Safety Tools

Signing experiences, simulations, alerts, and transaction-risk dashboards.

Developer Infrastructure

Explorers, analytics, monitoring, RPC tooling, deployment, and testing services.

Consumer Web3 Products

Marketplaces, payments, memberships, gaming, and accessible user-facing applications.

Risk Monitoring

Tools that use transparent on-chain signals to identify high-risk interactions.

AI-Powered Applications

Products combining smart contracts, AI inference, automation, and risk intelligence.
